The short version

Nordic Links runs on your own PC. Speech recognition and the caddie's voice work offline. We never receive your voice, your recordings or your screen. We collect as little as we can.

What the app does on your PC

  • Your voice is turned into text on your PC and is never sent anywhere.
  • The app reads the GSPro window on your screen to find distance, lie and club. Screen images stay on your PC.
  • Settings, players, bags and logs are stored locally in %APPDATA%\NordicLinks.

What leaves your PC

  • License check (Polar): when you activate or validate your license, the app sends your license key and a neutral PC label (a short code, not your PC or user name) to Polar.
  • Update check: the app fetches getnordiclinks.com/version.json (hosted on GitHub Pages).
  • Speed pack (optional): downloaded from PyPI (files.pythonhosted.org) if you choose it.
  • Error reports (from version 1.0.1, Sentry): if the app crashes or hits an error, a technical error report (error type, stack trace, app version, Windows version) is sent to Sentry, stored in the EU. File paths, PC name, user name, speech, screenshots and local variables are removed before sending. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Problem reports: only if you click "Report a problem" and email the file to us. It contains app logs (including recognized commands), your screen setup and, if you choose, a screenshot of the GSPro window.

Buying Nordic Links

Payments are handled by Polar Software, Inc., our merchant of record. Polar processes your name, email, country and payment details under its own privacy policy (https://polar.sh/legal/privacy). We see your email, country and order, and use them to deliver your license and support.

Email and beta sign-ups

If you email us or signed up for the beta, we keep your name, email and what you told us (e.g. launch monitor, country) to answer you and to tell you about Nordic Links. Ask us and we'll delete it.

Our website

getnordiclinks.com is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log your IP address for security. We use no cookies, no analytics and no ads.

Legal basis and storage

We process data to deliver what you bought (contract), to keep the app working and secure (legitimate interest) and to comply with accounting law. Order data is kept as long as Norwegian accounting law requires. Error reports are kept for at most 90 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Email post@getnordiclinks.com. You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet, datatilsynet.no).
