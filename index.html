Smart caddie

Ask what to hit. It reads the hole for you.

Say “Caddie, what club?” and it reads the distance, slope and lie straight from GSPro, works out how the shot plays, and picks the club from the bag of the player who is up. It switches bags when GSPro moves to the next player.

Distance to pin89.9 m
Slope+4.7 m uphill
LieDeep rough · 70 %
Plays like135 m → 7 iron

From a bad lie it never leaves you short, and it never hands you a wood out of deep rough. You can also tell it yourself:

“180 to target, 5 downhill”slope
“bunker, 45 to pin”lie
“150 yards, headwind 4”wind
“… and 3 meters up”add-on

Under the hood
0.07secondsspeech to text

Measured on a sim PC with an RTX 5070. Without a graphics card it still works, at about one second per command.

Your voice stays home

Speech is recognised on your own PC. No audio is sent anywhere.

Any USB mic

A desk mic two metres from the tee works fine.

Every player’s bag

Uses each player’s GSPro bag and switches when the turn changes.

Any launch monitor

It talks to GSPro itself, so it works with whatever you hit into.

Requirements: Windows PC running GSPro and a microphone. An NVIDIA graphics card is recommended for the fastest response.

FAQ

Questions

Does it cost anything?

The beta is free. Pricing will be announced at launch, and beta testers get an early price.

Does my voice leave my PC?

No. Speech recognition runs locally on your sim PC. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

How does it know the distance and lie?

It reads the player box GSPro already shows on screen: distance to the pin, slope and lie. You can always say them yourself instead.

Which launch monitors are supported?

Any launch monitor that works with GSPro. The voice control talks to GSPro directly, not to your launch monitor.

Does it work on Mac?

No. It runs on the same Windows PC as GSPro.

What language do I speak?

English golf terms: “seven iron”, “sand wedge”, “aim left”. Accents are fine.
